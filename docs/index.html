Overview

[ Coresearch ]

Find the right professors, and a PhD direction you can defend.

An open-source skill for your AI coding agent, with an app that runs on your computer. It reads professors’ papers, ranks them by the kinds of fit you choose, drafts pitches you send yourself, and tests research directions until only defensible ones survive. Every claim it makes is a quoted cutout you can check.

Install

You need Node.js 24.14 or newer (check with node -v), Git, and an AI agent that can run commands on your computer: Claude Code in the terminal, the desktop app, or an IDE, or Codex, Cursor, and similar. Browser-only chats cannot run it.

  1. Add the skill

    Claude Code: paste this into a session.

    Install the Coresearch skill from https://github.com/praveen221/coresearch into ~/.claude/skills/coresearch, then set it up.

    Codex, Cursor, and other agents: clone it, then tell your agent “Read ~/coresearch/SKILL.md and set up Coresearch.”

    git clone https://github.com/praveen221/coresearch ~/coresearch

  2. Choose your folder

    Your agent installs dependencies (about a minute), asks where to keep your research (any folder, such as ~/Documents/Coresearch), builds the app (one to two minutes), and opens it at http://127.0.0.1:3780.

  3. Start with yourself

    Tell your agent about yourself and upload your CV on the profile page. Add professors by name, or paste a Google Sheet link shared as “Anyone with the link can view”. Then ask it to rank them, draft pitches, or start testing research directions with a professor.

  4. Come back any day

    Open your agent in your research folder and say what you want; the app shows progress live. If the app is not running, say “start Coresearch”. To update, say “update Coresearch”; your research is never changed. To remove it, delete the skill folder; your research folder stays yours.

Costs, reasoning settings, and troubleshooting
Cost
Each research guess takes about half a million tokens and 40 minutes of agent time; a batch of 20 is 10 to 15 million tokens. Batches start only when you approve them.
Reasoning
Use your agent’s strongest setting for drafting, investigating, and reviewing; lighter settings are fine for your profile, lists, and pitch edits.
Errors about node:sqlite
Upgrade to Node.js 24.14 or newer.
Port already in use
Say “start Coresearch on port 3790”.
Paper lookups stop for the day
OpenAlex has a daily allowance; set OPENALEX_API_KEY to a free key from openalex.org.
Sharing with a collaborator
Export a report anyone can open from the Share page, or give live access over Tailscale (see the README).

Privacy

  • [x] Your CV and research stay in a folder you choose, on your computer.
  • [x] No account, no server, no tracking in the app.
  • [x] It never contacts anyone; you send every email and write the proposal.
  • [x] It checks each programme’s rules on AI help and holds drafts where AI help is banned.
