Find a PhD direction you can defend.

Your agent reads professors’ papers, ranks them by the fit you care about, drafts outreach you send yourself, and tests research directions until only defensible ones survive. Every claim quotes its source.

Codex, Cursor, and other agents

git clone https://github.com/praveen221/coresearch

Then tell your agent: “Read coresearch/SKILL.md and set up Coresearch.”

Needs Node.js 24.14+ and Git. Research uses about half a million tokens per guess; use your agent’s strongest reasoning setting.
